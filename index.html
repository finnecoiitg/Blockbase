About the Course

BlockBase 2026 is an 8-day blockchain course run by the Finance and Economics Club at IIT Guwahati.

It's built for people with no prior background in blockchain or Web3. The course starts from first principles — what problems blockchains solve and why they're structured the way they are — and builds toward writing and deploying your own decentralized application. No background in finance or cryptography required.

Over 8 days, you'll cover blockchain fundamentals, Ethereum, Solidity smart contracts, and full-stack dApp development. The final day is a project: a working, deployed dApp built entirely by you.

Each day's content is released on a fixed schedule as the course progresses.
